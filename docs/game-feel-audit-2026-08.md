# Game-feel audit — every in-game screen (2026-08-23)
Owner brief: "check every page and see what we can add/remove to make it more interesting like a game and remove noise at the same time — look like a game instead of looking like dashboards." Method: eleven parallel code auditors (one per screen, six-test lens: verb / fiction / stakes / consequence / aspiration / noise) + a live walkthrough of a week-37 Simulation save. GameShell (top bar + rail) was out of scope — page content only.

## 0. What the owner's walkthrough saw (week-37 Simulation save, desktop 1366)

Observed live before the code audit, and confirming its patterns:

- **The Founder's Notes banner is the loudest dashboard-ism in the product.** A full paragraph of
  tutorial prose renders at the top of EVERY screen, every week, above the page's own content.
  It has a dismiss ("Got it") but returns with each new note. It should become a one-line whisper,
  or move its lessons into the moments they describe (Pattern B below is the same finding).
- **The flash bar persists across screens** — "Hiring: Wei Moreau declined · Quinn Ivanov
  accepted" rode along to Growth, Product, Finance, Market and Story until the next advance.
  A flash should live on the HQ stream and die on navigation.
- **Rival name collision (bug, small but confusing):** the rival generator produced a rival named
  "Nimbus Labs" in a run whose player company is "Nimbus Labs" — the leaderboard showed the name
  at #1 (rival, Series A) and #4 ("(you)"). The generator should exclude the player's company name.
- The editorial voice (HQ's "Biggest uncertainty", Cohorts' "judge it over six weeks") is the
  game's strongest existing device — the audit's "verdict sentence" fix is asking to spread what
  already works.
# Founder Mode — "Game, not Dashboard" Synthesis
*Synthesized from eleven per-screen audits (dashboard-hq, growth, product-build, discovery, cohorts, hiring, team, fundraising, finance, market-rivals, story).*

---

## 1. Cross-screen patterns

**Pattern A — Numbers nobody speaks.** Bare stat tiles recur on almost every screen: the StatCard row with faux buttons (Dashboard.tsx:585-644), Finance's MacroPanel weather-widget trio (Finance.tsx:32-48), Market's share StatCard (Market.tsx:158-167), Fundraising's "Bar for next stage" tile (Fundraising.tsx:1119-1125), Cohorts' ±spread and WoW blocks (CohortAnalytics.tsx:500-507), Team's "pts/wk" engine-unit row. **The fix device: the verdict sentence with a face.** Every number either gets spoken by a named character (Ingrid, the banker, the board chair, the craftsman engineer — the living-world roster already exists in all three modes) or compressed into a judgment sentence the way Cohorts' verdict line already does ("That move is smaller than the gap between your own cohorts"). A tile that can't be voiced or judged gets deleted.

**Pattern B — The manual glued to the face.** Permanent tutorial prose re-rendered every week forever: Discovery's three intro lectures, Product's S-curve pitch and gauge legend, Hiring's four-line explainer and tip footer, Cohorts' triangle reading guide, Team's raise-rule caption, Market's war-room rules paragraphs, the Career panel footers ("People remember what you said" — every week, forever). **The fix device: rules ride the verb.** Teach a rule exactly once (first render or Disclosure), then put the live computed fact on the button itself — "Confirm — $5,200 severance, morale −8" replaces both the header caption and the lecture. The moment of the decision is the only classroom the game needs.

**Pattern C — One number, five homes.** Founder energy renders in five places on the HQ; PMF has four homes on one screen; the segment ceiling is stated three times on Discovery; TAM three times on Market; term-sheet expiry three times on Fundraising. **The fix device: the single-home rule, and the home is where the lever is.** A number lives beside the verb that moves it; everywhere else it appears only when it becomes the binding constraint (the hero pattern the HQ already got right).

**Pattern D — Silent consequences.** The player pulls a lever and no screen ever says what it did: Growth shows no receipt for last week's budget, Product shows no build output, Hiring declines evaporate entirely, Cohorts' chart has no decision markers, Team morale has no direction, and outside Career the HQ never explains the week. **The fix device: the receipt.** One line joining existing history/journal state to the screen — "Wk 14: you spent $8k — users 4,210 → 4,580 (+370)". `game.history`, `career.journal`, and the inbox already remember everything; the screens just never quote them.

**Pattern E — Present-tense-only rendering.** Screens state what is, never what's coming: no do-nothing projection on the hero, no drain line on the cohort chart, no strike-window on hostile rivals, no next milestone anywhere (MILESTONES with written flavor sits unrendered in engine.ts:1042-1114). **The fix device: the countdown and the finish line.** Project the current rate to a dated outcome ("cash hits zero in week 43 — two weeks after the board review") and surface the first unachieved milestone with distance ("1,000 users — 371 to go"). Both are pure arithmetic on state that already exists.

## 2. Top 10 moves (impact-per-effort, ranked)

1. **Hero "do-nothing" forecast — dashboard-hq, S.** The biggest number on the screen ends its sentence with the date of your own death: "At this net, cash hits zero in week 43 — two weeks after the board review."
2. **Net verdict line under the budget slider — growth, S.** The player drags the slider until "≈ net +N users/wk" flips green, turning the screen's one lever into a week you visibly win or lose — from two numbers already three inches apart.
3. **"If the review ran today" board verdict — fundraising, S.** The deadline becomes a game clock the player watches flip from STRIKE 2-of-3 to PASS as their week lands, using the exact predicates `boardReview` already runs.
4. **Live Draw/Repay readout — finance, S.** "This draw: +9 weeks of runway, costs $860/wk interest" appears while the finger is still on the slider — the moment a bank form becomes a game. (Ship the covenant fix with it: the flat "15%" fine print is factually stale against `covenantConversion` — that's a lie, not just noise.)
5. **Candidate acceptance mood — hiring, S.** "Nervous about your runway" appears on the card *before* the blind Send-offer click, so the player fixes runway first instead of learning from the decline in the Inbox.
6. **Next-milestone chip on HQ + Growth — S.** "1,000 users — 371 to go" makes the 🏁 inbox celebration a payoff the player was chasing instead of a surprise; one engine table (already flavored) feeds both screens in all three modes.
7. **Price on the destructive verbs — team, S.** Fire arms inline to "Confirm — $5,200 severance, team morale −8" (killing the fourth-wall-breaking `window.confirm`), and Raise previews "$68k → $75k · morale 41 → 53" on the button itself.
8. **Belief fog-of-war bars — discovery, S, career-only.** Each hypothesis renders its lo-hi uncertainty band as a physical grey fog that visibly narrows when a study lands — running an experiment finally *does* something on the board you're looking at (`beliefBand` already computes it, belief-safe).
9. **Territory bar — market-rivals, M.** The market becomes a contested map — your slice, named rivals' slices, shrinking greenfield — replacing two StatCards and absorbing three cuts as it lands.
10. **"The next chapter" epilogue — story, M.** A live run's biography ends on a cliffhanger with a name and a due date ("Maya Chen is waiting on the two people you promised — due week 41") instead of trailing off at the present, and next week's beat pays it off on the same screen.

*Just missed: Cohorts' anticipation strip ("your wk-31 cohort reports in 2 weeks" — S, and it makes window-churn stop feeling like the game lying), Product's PMF momentum arrow (S), Market's rap-sheet/trophy/strike-window chip trio (all S, all existing fields).*

## 4. Verdict

Founder Mode currently sits at about 6.4/10 on the game↔dashboard spectrum, and unevenly: the *top halves* of screens already play like a game — the binding-constraint hero, the sealed-bid hiring auction, the armed pivot button, term sheets with clocks, a biography with chapters — while the *bottom thirds* are still SaaS residue: stat tiles nobody speaks, manuals glued to panel faces, and levers whose consequences vanish the moment they're pulled. The engine is dramatically ahead of the presentation — milestones with written flavor, rival rap sheets, decline reasons, promise ledgers, and shock records all sit in state unrendered — so the redesign is mostly excavation, not invention. The sentence that should guide it: **every number on screen must be spoken by someone, end in a date, or open a door — anything that just sits there is furniture, and furniture is what dashboards are made of.**
